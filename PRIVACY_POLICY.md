# ORBIT HOP Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 9, 2026**

ORBIT HOP is a portrait arcade game for players aged 13 and older. This notice describes the current Android app and its optional integrations. It must be hosted at a public HTTPS URL and linked from the Play listing before release. The publisher must keep the Play listing's developer contact address current so players can send privacy requests.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores its offline profile in Android app storage: language and accessibility/audio settings; local score, run, mission, achievement and level progress; earned virtual currency; equipped and owned cosmetics/upgrades; daily reward dates; and stable purchase-grant IDs needed to avoid duplicate local fulfillment (the raw Play purchase token is sent only to the configured verifier and is not saved in the local profile). The normal profile includes a last-known-good recovery copy. It is not uploaded as a save file, and it is not encrypted by this app. Android app data is removed when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared. Android cloud backup is disabled in the current manifest.

The game does not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone. No account is required for offline play. There is no in-app chat, public user-generated content, or multiplayer matchmaking.

## Optional services

**Advertising and consent.** Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) are included. No banner ads are placed. A rewarded video is shown only after the player chooses a reward action; interstitials can appear only after a completed game-over screen and follow the session/run/time limits described in the game. UMP determines whether ads may be requested and exposes privacy options when required. If ads are requested, Google may process device/advertising identifiers, IP address, app and device information, ad interactions, and consent signals to provide, measure, limit, and protect ads. Ads are configured for a 13+ audience; the app does not target children. The exact data processed depends on region, consent, device controls, and Google's policies.

**Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.** These services are disabled by default in the app. A player must turn on “Crash reports & gameplay analytics” in Settings before this app enables event/crash collection. When enabled, Firebase may receive gameplay events (such as run start/end, score, duration, ad format, and product ID), crash reports, app version, diagnostic information, and app/device identifiers. The app does not send the local save file or directly collected contact details. Firebase Console retention and deletion settings are controlled by the publisher.

**Google Play Games.** Sign-in, score submission, and online leaderboards are optional. If used, Google Play Games processes the player's Play Games profile/identifier and submitted scores under Google's terms and privacy policy. Local leaderboards remain available without sign-in.

**Google Play Billing and purchase verification.** Google Play processes the payment and may return a purchase token, product ID, and order/purchase ID. When an HTTPS verification endpoint is configured, ORBIT HOP sends the package name, product ID, purchase token, and purchase ID to the publisher's verifier. The verifier validates the purchase with Google Play, prevents duplicate grants, and returns an entitlement result. The publisher must restrict access to purchase records, retain them only as long as necessary for entitlement, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal obligations, and provide a contact route for deletion requests where legally possible. No payment-card or bank-account details are collected by the game.

**Score-card sharing.** The app renders a score image locally and opens Android's system share sheet only when the player taps Share. The player chooses whether and where to send it. ORBIT HOP does not operate a sharing service or receive a copy of the shared image.

## Purposes and legal bases

Optional integrations support advertising and consent management, online scores, purchase fulfillment and fraud prevention, and—only after opt-in—gameplay analytics and crash diagnosis. Offline game functionality and local scores do not require these integrations. Consent choices may be changed in Android/Google settings and through in-app Settings/UMP privacy choices where available. Some purchase processing is necessary to deliver a requested transaction.

## Sharing, security, and retention

Data is shared only with the selected optional provider or the publisher's purchase-verification service for the purposes above; it is not sold by ORBIT HOP. Online requests use platform HTTPS/TLS. Local game data remains in app-private storage; it is not cloud-synced by this app. Google and Firebase process data under their own terms and privacy notices. The publisher should configure provider retention controls, secure service credentials, and monitor access to the verifier.

## Children and audience

The game is designed for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. It does not use child-directed ad treatment. The publisher must answer Play Console age-audience, content-rating, and ad disclosures consistently with the released SDKs and enabled features.

## Your choices and requests

You can play without signing in, disable analytics/crash collection in Settings, decline or change ad consent where UMP offers those choices, skip every rewarded ad, and use the device's Android settings to reset or uninstall the app. For access, correction, or deletion requests concerning online service or purchase records, use the **Contact developer** details on the ORBIT HOP Google Play listing. The publisher must keep that address monitored and respond under applicable law. Removing local app storage by clearing data or uninstalling deletes the profile from the device; deleting the profile does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase.

## Changes

The publisher may update this policy when SDKs, data flows, or legal requirements change. The effective date above should be updated with each material revision. The Play listing must link to the currently applicable hosted version.
